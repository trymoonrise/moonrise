Report and remove content

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. What you can report

Donation notes and published websites are written by users. Report a note or a site if it should come down.

2. What is not allowed

Do not publish illegal content, someone else's copyrighted work, private data you do not have rights to, or anything that impersonates a business you do not represent.

3. How to ask for removal

Email trymoonrise@gmail.com with the page address, what is wrong, and how to reach you. Moonrise reviews the report and removes donation notes and published sites that break these rules.

4. Copyright

The designated agent for copyright complaints is Moonrise Studio, at trymoonrise@gmail.com. Send the work you own, the Moonrise URL, and a statement that the use is not authorized. The Copyright Office directory is at copyright.gov/dmca-directory.

5. Contact

Questions about a report: trymoonrise@gmail.com or (401) 300-0957.
