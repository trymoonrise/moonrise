Loaders

Tell people the system is working. Match the loader to wait time and layout.

Spinner

Short waits under a few seconds. Keep it next to the action that triggered it.

Skeleton

Use when content shape is known. Reduces layout jump better than a lone spinner.

Progress bar

Best when you can estimate completion. Always show a percent or step label.
