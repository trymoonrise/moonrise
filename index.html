What is Moonrise?

FAQ

What is Moonrise Studio?

An AI studio that helps creators build professional websites for local businesses and earn when clients unlock the live site.

How does Moonrise work?

Find businesses that need a site, build their preview for free, send the link, and get paid when the owner unlocks the watermark.

How much does it cost to go live?

Creators build free. Owners pay on the watermarked site. You keep 90%. Moonrise keeps 10%. The watermark drops after payment.

Can owners pay without signing up?

Yes. They pay on the live site through Stripe. They do not need a creator account.
