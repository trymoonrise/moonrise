3. Categories of information we collect

3.1 Account and profile information

When you register or update Settings, we may collect display name, handle, email address, an optional passkey, profile photo, communication preferences, session preferences (such as remember-me), and other details you choose to store in your profile. Moonrise never asks for a password, a government-issued ID, a driver’s license, a passport, photo or face recognition, a Social Security number, or similar identity documents. A profile photo is not an ID scan or face recognition. Sign-in uses an email link. A passkey is optional. The one-time Employee ID used to create an account is a signup code, not a government ID.

3.2 Workspace and project information

We may store business names, addresses, phone numbers, map links, notes, categories, Website Price and related pricing selections, website HTML and project files, publish status, watermark status, QR business-card links, redesign history, upload or GitHub import metadata, and other content you enter, generate, edit, upload, or save while using Builder, Editor, Projects, or related tools.

3.3 Contact form and custom domain settings

Contact-form delivery and custom-domain connection are optional and enabled only when you turn them on. If enabled, we may store whether the feature is on, notification email or custom endpoint URL, domain hostname, DNS or verification status from hosting providers, and related configuration.

3.4 Lead and Business Finder activity

We may process search queries, place or category filters, saved leads, status markers, and related workspace actions. Some lead information reflects publicly available business details or data from third-party sources. We do not guarantee accuracy or completeness of that information.

3.5 Published-site and form submission information

If a creator enables Moonrise contact-form delivery, submissions may include names, phone numbers, messages, and other fields on the form. Those submissions may be stored in our systems and/or emailed or forwarded to the destination the creator configured.

3.6 Business-owner checkout information

When an owner pays through the watermark, we may process payment status, Stripe checkout or session identifiers, invoice or receipt email if provided, project identifiers, and limited billing metadata needed to unlock the site and credit the creator. Full card numbers and sensitive payment credentials are handled by Stripe, not stored by Moonrise in full.

3.7 Creator payout and earnings information

We may store revenue-share records, Dashboard income figures, payout method details you provide (for example, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, or other methods you enter), donation status, Store purchases, and related transaction identifiers.

3.8 Creator contact shown on watermarked sites

Display name, handle, email, or phone you add for client contact may be embedded on watermarked published sites so owners can reach you about changes. Moonrise HQ contact details may also appear for billing and platform support.

3.9 Support and correspondence

Emails, help requests, phone communications, Discord or Telegram messages you send us, and related records may be retained to resolve your request and improve support quality.

3.10 Technical, device, and usage data

We may collect IP address, device and browser type, approximate location derived from network data, pages and features used, timestamps, diagnostic logs, error reports, performance metrics, cookies or similar storage, and security events. This helps us operate, secure, debug, and improve the Service.

Product fonts are self-hosted from Moonrise (see css/fonts.css and assets/fonts/). We do not load fonts from Google Fonts or similar third-party font CDNs, so those providers do not receive your IP address from Moonrise font requests. When Business Finder or other map UI loads, map libraries or tile providers (for example MapLibre via a CDN, or OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap-derived tiles) may receive your IP address and related technical data under their own terms. Payment, auth, hosting, and AI providers listed in this Policy may also receive technical data needed to run those features.

3.11 Optional push notifications

If you enable browser push notifications, we may store a push subscription endpoint and related metadata needed to deliver messages you requested.

If you believe your account has been compromised, contact trymoonrise@gmail.com promptly. To the fullest extent permitted by law, Moonrise is not liable for unauthorized access resulting from your failure to protect credentials or devices.

We try to minimize what would identify a Moonrise creator if there is a data breach. Moonrise never asks for a password, a government-issued ID, a driver’s license, a passport, photo or face recognition, a Social Security number, or similar identity documents, so those are not records we hold. We do not store full payment card numbers. If account data were exposed, these are the records that can be linked to you:

  • Account email, username, display name, profile photo, and account id.
  • Phone number and country, if you added one.
  • Session records and sign-in security events that can be tied to an email address or IP address. A passkey, if you add one, can be linked to the account. Moonrise does not ask for or store a password.
  • Short-lived email sign-in links, while they are still valid.
  • Notification preferences, when the account was created or updated, and whether the account is frozen.
  • The contact email and phone you save for clients, including when those details are copied onto a published site.
  • Payout method and the handle, email, or phone you enter for Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, Zelle, or another method, plus earnings and payout amounts and status.
  • Card brand, last four digits, expiration, and a card fingerprint if you verify a security card. Full card numbers stay with Stripe.
  • Credit plan, credit balance, credit history, Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, donation or support status, and Store purchases.
  • Projects you save: business name, address, phone, map link, notes, category, website price, site HTML and files, publish URL, and watermark status.
  • Generation prompts, generated site HTML, and error messages from those jobs.
  • Contact-form settings and form submissions on your projects, including a sender’s name, phone, and message, and the notification email or endpoint you configured.
  • Custom domain hostname and DNS or verification status, if you connect a domain.
  • Clients you add by hand: business name, phone, website, price, and notes.
  • GitHub username and GitHub account id, plus the access token, if you connect GitHub.
  • Calendar username and connected account id, access tokens, and synced bookings (title, time, guest name, email, phone, and meeting link), if you connect a calendar.
  • Browser push subscription and the browser used for it, if you turn notifications on.
  • IP address, device and browser type, approximate location from network data, pages used, and timestamps in logs tied to your account.
  • Payments on your projects: amount, status, and Stripe payment identifiers. Those records also connect you to the business that paid.
  • An employee signup redemption, if one was used on the account.

If we confirm a breach of personal account data, we email affected account holders at the address on the account from trymoonrise@gmail.com. We watch for issues through production logs (including Vercel and Supabase) and Stripe signals. We aim to investigate promptly and notify without undue delay, and within 72 hours of confirming a breach that requires notice under applicable law, unless a lawful request asks us to wait. Our internal runbook is also summarized in SECURITY.md.
