Getting started

Moonrise is a workspace for selling websites to local businesses. Find a business that needs a site, build it in Studio, publish a watermarked preview, and earn when the owner pays on that page to go live.

  • Free to build — create, preview, redesign, and publish watermarked sites without charging the owner up front.
  • They pay on the watermark — the live site stays watermarked until the owner completes Moonrise checkout.
  • You keep 90% — Moonrise keeps 10% of each go-live payment (before processor fees and adjustments under the Terms).

What is Moonrise?

Moonrise Studio is an AI-powered platform that helps creators build professional websites for local businesses and earn when those businesses go live. You find a business that needs a site, generate a polished preview, customize it, publish it with a Moonrise watermark, and share the live link. The business owner pays on the watermark checkout. When they pay, the watermark is removed automatically and you earn your creator share.

  • Creators / sellers — find local businesses, build sites, and earn when owners unlock.
  • Business owners — open the live preview link and pay on the watermark. They usually do not need a creator account to pay.
  • Typical customers include plumbers, salons, restaurants, cleaners, contractors, clinics, and other local service businesses.

How Moonrise works

The product loop is short and repeatable.

  1. FindNearby businesses that need a site
  2. BuildGenerate and polish in Studio
  3. PublishShare the watermarked live URL
  4. PayOwner unlocks on the watermark
  5. EarnYou receive 90% via your payout method

Moonrise hosts the preview, runs watermark checkout, and splits the go-live payment. You own the sales conversation.

  1. Find Open Business Finder or paste a Maps link in Builder. Prefer active, under-served businesses.
  2. Generate Slide-to-generate in Finder, or fill details in Builder. Wait until the preview appears. Generation is free for creators.
  3. Polish & price Open the project in Editor. Fix copy and media. Set Website Price before you publish.
  4. Publish & share Publish the watermarked live site and send the owner that URL—not only a local preview.
  5. Get paid When they pay on the watermark, Moonrise removes it, redeploys a clean site, and credits your share. Keep payout details current in Settings.

Golden rules

1. Creators do not charge business owners for go-live. Build, publish, and share the live site link. The owner pays only through the Moonrise watermark.

2. Set Website Price before you publish. That is what the owner sees at checkout. You keep 90%; Moonrise keeps 10% of that go-live amount (subject to processor fees and Terms).

3. Do not remove or hide the watermark before the owner pays. Do not tell them to pay you instead of Moonrise checkout.

4. Optional features stay off until you enable them. Contact form, custom domain, and other widgets are manual toggles—not required to publish.

Breaking the payment rules can lead to permanent ban, withheld payouts, site removal, and other consequences described in the Terms of Service.

How you get paid

Publish the website, then wait for the customer to pay through the built-in watermark checkout. Do not collect payment outside Moonrise—Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, or other third-party methods—for the same unlock. After they pay on the watermark, Moonrise processes the payment and delivers your earnings through the payout method you set in Settings.

  • Checkout is powered by Stripe on the live watermark page.
  • Owners do not need a Moonrise creator account to pay.
  • Go-live checkout may also describe hosting or recurring charges—show those details to the owner if they ask. Owners can manage hosting later from Manage hosting.
  • Dashboard income reflects your 90% creator share. You are responsible for taxes on your earnings.

Business Finder

Business Finder uses open map data from OpenStreetMap and OpenFreeMap to scan your local area for nearby cafés, clinics, shops, and similar businesses. Claim a lead that still needs a website, then generate their site in the Builder.

  • Slide to generate opens Builder and starts a site for that lead. Keep the slide held until it completes.

  • Lead data can come from public or third-party sources. Verify phone and business name before you publish or call.

User interface

Studio is organized into a few channels. Each has one job.

  • Dashboard — income, goals, and a snapshot of creator activity.
  • Business Finder — search, claim, and send leads into Builder.
  • Builder — start sites from a Google link, manual details, or a GitHub upload.
  • Editor — polish copy, toggle features, set Website Price, publish, and connect domains.
  • Projects — every site you have generated or imported.
  • My Clients — paid go-live businesses after watermark checkout.
  • Settings — profile, security, payout method, and account controls.
  • Course — creator walkthrough video and onboarding help.
  • UI Library — interactive patterns used in generated sites.

Builder modes

Open Builder and choose how to start. The three tabs match the product UI.

  • Google link — paste a Maps or g.page link in the Link field, then generate.
  • Manual — type business name, phone, category, address, and an optional short description.
  • Upload — connect GitHub, pick a repo and path, and import an existing site. After import, use the Moonrise watermark controls in the Editor sidebar.

Builder & Editor

Moonrise builds the site with one click. Reviewing in the editor is optional—you can make minor edits, turn features on or off, share the preview when it looks right, and update anytime.

  • Generate builds a full site from business details. Wait for the progress UI until the preview appears.
  • Preview — see the live layout. Edit — click sections to change text and media on the page. Code — view or edit HTML (may require MVP+ or an active Starter, Pro, or Business plan).
  • Redesign creates a fresh visual direction on an existing project.
  • Device previews — check desktop, tablet, and phone before you share.

Website Price

Website Price is what the business owner pays at watermark checkout. It is not a fee you collect personally. Set it before you publish. If you change it later, save and republish so checkout stays accurate.

  • You keep 90%; Moonrise keeps 10% of the go-live amount (before processor fees and adjustments under the Terms).
  • Accepting cash, Venmo, PayPal, invoices, or other off-platform payment for go-live unlock is prohibited.

Contact Owner

Use Contact Owner in the Editor sidebar to call the business phone on the project. If the number is missing, add it under Settings → Contact (or from the lead details) before calling.

Separately, the watermark on a live preview can show your creator contact details so the owner can reach you about edits. Keep phone and email current in Settings.

Editor tools

  • Download HTML — export the current site HTML (plan gate may apply, same as View Code).
  • QR Code — open the QR business-card modal to copy the live link or download the QR image (publish first for a public URL).
  • Flyer — printable website flyer with a scan-to-visit QR; print or download from the flyer modal.
  • Edit website manually — switch to Edit mode, click sections on the page, change copy/media, then Done.
  • View code — switch to Code mode to inspect or edit HTML when your plan unlocks it.
  • Unpublish — takes the public link offline until you publish again (confirm in the dialog).
  • Delete project — permanently removes the site, preview, and publish record. This cannot be undone.

Site features

Optional widgets stay off until you turn them on in the Editor. Turning a feature on or off saves it; use Save & apply, and if the site is already live that update publishes immediately. Features do not replace Website Price / watermark checkout.

Contact form

  • Open the project in Editor and expand Contact Form.
  • Turn the toggle on. Choose Auto (email notifications) or Custom (your own endpoint).
  • Enter the notification email or endpoint URL, then Save & apply.
  • If the site is already live, republish/update so delivery activates.
  • Send test email is available after Auto mode is saved with a valid notification email.

Custom domains

By default, sites deploy on Vercel with a free *.vercel.app address for quick sharing. After the client pays for go-live, we recommend purchasing a custom domain for them. You can also connect a domain they already own.

  • Publish the site first so there is a live deployment to attach.
  • In Editor, expand Custom Domain, turn the toggle on, pick the domain service, enter the hostname, and save.
  • Follow the DNS steps Moonrise shows, then click Check status.
  • DNS changes can take a few minutes (sometimes longer) to verify.

DNS records Moonrise expects

  • Apex domain (example.com): A record @ → 76.76.21.21, plus optional CNAME www → cname.vercel-dns.com.
  • Subdomain: CNAME → cname.vercel-dns.com.
  • Remove conflicting A or CNAME records for the same name before adding Moonrise values.
  • On Cloudflare, keep the proxy off (DNS only) while verifying.

Booking

  • In Editor, open the Booking System widget and turn it on.
  • Enable a provider and paste its link: Cal.com, Square Appointments, Calendly, Acuity, or Setmore.
  • If the generated site already included a booking link, the widget may prefill—review and correct it.
  • Save & apply. On a live site, the update publishes with the save.

Payment methods

The Payments widget tells visitors how they can pay the business (tips, invoices, store links). It does not replace Website Price watermark checkout for go-live unlock.

  • Enable Payments in the Editor.
  • Turn on providers the business already uses—Stripe Payment Links, Square, PayPal, Venmo, or Cash App—and paste each URL.
  • Save & apply, then confirm on Preview that the links open correctly.

Quotes

  • Enable Quote / Leads when the business wants a request-a-quote CTA.
  • Connect a form provider such as Tally, Typeform, Jotform, Formspree, or Google Forms.
  • Save & apply and test from Preview before sharing with the client.

Maps

  • Enable Maps when the site should show a location.
  • Add an address or link for Google Maps, Apple Maps, Mapbox, or Waze.
  • If Finder already supplied an address, review it and correct typos before publish.

Reviews

  • Enable Reviews and link the owner’s public pages (Google Maps, Yelp, Trustpilot, or Facebook).
  • Do not invent ratings. Prefer links the business already owns.
  • Save & apply, then check Preview on mobile and desktop.

Socials

  • Enable Social and add the profiles the business wants public—Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Nextdoor, or Pinterest.
  • Use full profile URLs so buttons open correctly on every device.
  • Save & apply when the set looks complete.

Messaging

  • Enable Messaging for SMS, WhatsApp, phone call, Messenger, email, or Telegram.
  • Enter the phone, email, or chat URL carefully; test from a phone after publish.

Ordering

  • Enable Ordering when the business takes online orders through Square Online, Shopify, Toast, DoorDash, or a similar menu link.
  • Paste their ordering URL. This does not process go-live unlock payments.
  • Save & apply and verify the link on Preview.

Newsletter

  • Enable Newsletter and connect Mailchimp, Beehiiv, Substack, or another signup URL the business already uses.
  • Make sure the destination is owned by the client so they receive subscribers.
  • Save & apply before sharing the live site.

Security

Moonrise uses commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. You use the Service at your own risk and are responsible for protecting credentials and devices.

  • Production uses HTTPS with HSTS, plus security headers (CSP, frame protections, and related browser policies).
  • Studio API routes use CORS allowlisting, auth lockouts, and per-route rate limits. Authenticated routes require a valid Supabase Bearer token.
  • Stripe webhooks are verified with a signing secret. Secrets stay in host environment variables—not in client code.
  • Sign in with email-link or passkey where available. Keep recovery email access.
  • Go-live card data is handled by Stripe, not stored in full by Moonrise.

Tips & troubleshooting

  • Generation stuck? Wait for the progress UI. Avoid refreshing mid-job. Retry with clearer business details if it fails.
  • Owner cannot pay? Confirm they opened the live published URL and Website Price is set.
  • Contact form not emailing? Toggle on, Auto mode with a valid email, Save & apply, update the live site.
  • Domain still pending? Remove conflicting records, add Moonrise DNS, wait, then Check status. See Custom domains.
  • Code / Download HTML locked? Unlock with MVP+ or an active Starter, Pro, or Business plan.
  • QR or flyer empty? Publish first so there is a public URL to encode.
  • Contact Owner does nothing? Add the business phone on the project or in Settings → Contact.
