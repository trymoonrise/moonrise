This Privacy Policy explains how Moonrise Studio (“Moonrise,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use Moonrise Studio, including trymoonrise.com, related apps, dashboards, Business Finder, Builder and Editor, published websites we host or process, watermark checkout, payouts, donations, MVP+ features, and related support channels (collectively, the “Service”).

2. Who this Policy covers

This Policy applies to information we process about:

  • Creators and other registered users of the Service.
  • Visitors to our marketing and product pages.
  • Business owners who pay through watermark checkout or contact us about a published site.
  • People who submit contact forms on published sites when a creator enables Moonrise form delivery.

This Policy does not replace privacy notices you may need to provide on websites you create for clients. If you publish a site, collect form submissions, or process client information, you are responsible for lawful notices and consents for that activity.

3. Categories of information we collect

3.1 Account and profile information

When you register or update Settings, we may collect display name, handle, email address, password or passkey credentials, profile photo, communication preferences, session preferences (such as remember-me), and other details you choose to store in your profile.

3.2 Workspace and project information

We may store business names, addresses, phone numbers, map links, notes, categories, Website Price and related pricing selections, website HTML and project files, publish status, watermark status, QR business-card links, redesign history, upload or GitHub import metadata, and other content you enter, generate, edit, upload, or save while using Builder, Editor, Projects, or related tools.

3.3 Contact form and custom domain settings

Contact-form delivery and custom-domain connection are optional and enabled only when you turn them on. If enabled, we may store whether the feature is on, notification email or custom endpoint URL, domain hostname, DNS or verification status from hosting providers, and related configuration.

3.4 Lead and Business Finder activity

We may process search queries, place or category filters, saved leads, status markers, and related workspace actions. Some lead information reflects publicly available business details or data from third-party sources. We do not guarantee accuracy or completeness of that information.

3.5 Published-site and form submission information

If a creator enables Moonrise contact-form delivery, submissions may include names, phone numbers, messages, and other fields on the form. Those submissions may be stored in our systems and/or emailed or forwarded to the destination the creator configured.

3.6 Business-owner checkout information

When an owner pays through the watermark, we may process payment status, Stripe checkout or session identifiers, invoice or receipt email if provided, project identifiers, and limited billing metadata needed to unlock the site and credit the creator. Full card numbers and sensitive payment credentials are handled by Stripe, not stored by Moonrise in full.

3.7 Creator payout and earnings information

We may store revenue-share records, Dashboard income figures, payout method details you provide (for example, Cash App, Venmo, PayPal, or other methods you enter), MVP+ or donation status, Store purchases, and related transaction identifiers.

3.8 Creator contact shown on watermarked sites

Display name, handle, email, or phone you add for client contact may be embedded on watermarked published sites so owners can reach you about changes. Moonrise HQ contact details may also appear for billing and platform support.

3.9 Support and correspondence

Emails, help requests, phone communications, Discord or Telegram messages you send us, and related records may be retained to resolve your request and improve support quality.

3.10 Technical, device, and usage data

We may collect IP address, device and browser type, approximate location derived from network data, pages and features used, timestamps, diagnostic logs, error reports, performance metrics, cookies or similar storage, and security events. This helps us operate, secure, debug, and improve the Service.

3.11 Optional push notifications

If you enable browser push notifications, we may store a push subscription endpoint and related metadata needed to deliver messages you requested.
