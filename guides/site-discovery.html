Guides

Search, AI answers, and install

Moonrise optimizes public pages and generated client sites for people, search engines, answer engines, and home-screen install. It does not invent reviews, ratings, or awards.

SEO

Titles, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, robots rules, sitemaps, and LocalBusiness or Organization structured data. Client sites use the live published URL after go-live publish.

GEO

Name, phone, address, hours, map links, and geo place tags when those facts exist. City and region come from the provided address. Missing facts stay out.

AEO

Short direct answers, FAQ blocks when present, Speakable selectors, and clear page jobs so answer engines can quote the page.

LLMO

Public Moonrise facts live in llms.txt and llms-full.txt. Published client sites get their own llms.txt built from the same business facts on the project.

ASO

Moonrise is an installable web app, not a separate App Store listing. Install from Download. The manifest name is Moonrise Studio. The short name is Moonrise.

Social SEO

Open Graph and Twitter cards for titles, descriptions, and preview images when a page is shared in chat apps or social feeds.

Limits

None of this guarantees rankings, citations, installs, or sales. Studio pages stay private and should not be cited as public product docs.
