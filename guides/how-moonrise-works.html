How Moonrise works

Creators build for free. Owners pay on the live watermark. Creators keep 90%.

  1. Find

    Open Business Finder for nearby shops and clinics, or paste a Google Maps link in Builder.

  2. Build

    Generate the site. Preview, edit, and publish a watermarked page without paying Moonrise.

  3. Share

    Send the owner the live URL. They do not need a Studio account.

  4. Get paid

    The owner pays on the watermark checkout. Moonrise removes the watermark and credits the creator 90% of that go-live amount.

Price and split

The creator sets Website Price before publishing. A common price is about $500. Moonrise keeps 10% before processor fees. Full rules live in Terms.
