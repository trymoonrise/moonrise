Guides

Sell websites to local businesses

Moonrise is built for creators who sell one site at a time to a nearby shop, salon, clinic, cafe, or contractor.

Who buys

Owners who need a clear phone number, hours, address, and a way to get in touch. Prefer businesses that are open and still missing a usable site.

The sales loop

  1. Pick a lead

    Use Business Finder or a Maps link. Verify the name and phone before you call or message.

  2. Generate the preview

    Build the page in Studio. Keep the copy short. Set Website Price before you publish.

  3. Send the live link

    Share the watermarked URL, not only a screenshot. The owner can open it on their phone.

  4. Let checkout finish

    Do not collect go-live payment outside Moonrise. The owner pays on the watermark. You keep 90%.

Stay honest

Moonrise does not guarantee income, rankings, or lead quality. Creators own their outreach and client relationships. Product rules are in the Docs.
