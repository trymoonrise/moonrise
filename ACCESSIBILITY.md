# Accessibility

Moonrise Studio should work with a keyboard, a screen reader, and reduced motion.

## What we aim for

- Pages on [trymoonrise.com](https://trymoonrise.com) can be reached and read without a mouse.
- The control that has focus stays visible.
- Motion eases off when the device asks for reduced motion.
- Text and buttons keep enough contrast to read.
- Controls have names, including icon buttons.
- A preview, a form, and a checkout can be completed without a pointer.

## Report a barrier

Email [trymoonrise@gmail.com](mailto:trymoonrise@gmail.com) with:

- The page address
- What you were trying to do
- What got in the way
- The browser or assistive tool, if you know it

We read reports and fix barriers on the live product. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other people's private information.
