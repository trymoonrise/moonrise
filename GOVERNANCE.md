# Governance

Moonrise Studio is a product operated at [trymoonrise.com](https://trymoonrise.com).

## Who decides

The people who run Moonrise decide what ships. Notes from creators and from business owners are welcome. A suggestion does not transfer ownership of the product or of this repository.

## What this repository is

These pages explain how Moonrise works and how to reach the team. The product itself is the hosted studio. It is not offered as a download or as a project for someone else to host.

## How changes show up

A change appears on [trymoonrise.com](https://trymoonrise.com) and, when it is released, under [Releases](https://github.com/trymoonrise/moonrise/releases).

## How to raise something

- A problem while using the product: open an issue, or email [trymoonrise@gmail.com](mailto:trymoonrise@gmail.com). See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- Help with an account or an order: see [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
- A security concern: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post it in public.
- Conduct: see [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
